Research & Writings Education Experience

Welcome! I am a Master's student in Urban Studies and Practice at the Indian Institute for Human Settlements (IIHS).

My interests lie at the intersection of urban governance, public systems, planning, labour, migration, housing, civic participation, and urban transformation.

I am interested in understanding how institutions, infrastructure, communities, and different actors shape cities and influence everyday urban life. My academic work particularly explores questions around urban governance, participatory planning, informality, public systems, and the relationship between state and non-state actors.

I also have an interest in using data, mapping, digital tools, and civic technology to understand urban problems and explore possibilities for improving public systems.

My current academic journey builds on a background in Statistics and an expanding engagement with urban studies, governance, policy, and practice.

You can explore my research interests, writings, academic background, and experience below.

Research Interests

My research interests are centred around understanding how urban areas are governed, planned, produced, and experienced.

Urban Governance

Urban institutions, decentralisation, metropolitan governance, accountability, and relationships between different levels of government.

Urban Planning

Master planning, participatory planning, planning institutions, local knowledge, and the limitations of conventional planning frameworks.

Housing & Urban Informality

Housing production, affordability, informal settlements, tenure, welfare, and the involvement of non-state actors in housing.

Labour & Migration

Informal employment, migrant workers, labour conditions, housing, social integration, and access to welfare.

Public Systems

Institutional capacity, service delivery, digital governance, public administration, and citizen-oriented public systems.

Urban History & Heritage

Colonial urbanism, heritage, public memory, historical spaces, and the transformation of urban institutional landscapes.
